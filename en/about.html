who_we_are?

we are a volunteer collective. we are not a traditional bike shop. here you don't leave your bike and pay for labor hours. here you learn how to fix your own transportation with our help and tools.

we emerged from the need for a space where knowledge is shared, not sold. our mission is simple: the more bikes on the road, the less parts in the trash.
