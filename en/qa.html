frequently_asked_questions

how much does it cost to fix a bike?
using the space, tools and advice is free. if you use consumable materials (patches, chains, oil), you can leave a donation in the box. your donations pay for the space rental.
do I need to make an appointment?
no. just show up on Thursday between 18:00 and 20:00. if it's busy, grab a beer or coffee and wait for a table to become available.
I know nothing about bikes, will you help me?
absolutely. that's why we're here. we'll explain the process, show you which tools you need, and supervise you while you work. the goal is that the next time you'll know it yourself.
do you have spare parts?
we have a bunch of used parts that have been donated. if you need something specific or new (e.g., new tires or a special chain), it's best to buy it yourself and bring it along, and we'll help you install it.
can I leave my bike and come back for it tomorrow?
no. we don't have storage space. the bike goes with you the same day, whether it's fixed or not.
do you fix electric bikes?
no. we focus exclusively on bicycles (analog mechanics).
